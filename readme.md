**Project Proposal Name**
TaskMate


**Pitch**
      TaskMate is a productivity app designed to help students stay organized, manage their tasks efficiently, and reduce the stress that comes with juggling school, work, and personal responsibilities. By combining simple task tracking with user-friendly design, TaskMate helps users stay on top of what they need to do without feeling overwhelmed.



**Project Description**

      TaskMate is a task management application built specifically with students in mind. The app allows users to create, organize, and track tasks in one place, making it easier to plan their days and stay productive. During this phase of the project, the app will be launched and tested with real users to evaluate usability, functionality, and overall user experience.
      
      Based on user feedback and testing results, the app will be finalized by fixing issues, improving features, and ensuring smooth performance. Once the product reaches a polished state with positive user feedback, the team will present the final version of TaskMate to stakeholders, highlighting its features, benefits, and development process.



**Primary Users**
      
      College and university students
      
      Students balancing multiple classes, deadlines, and extracurricular activities
      
      Users who want a simple, easy-to-use task management tool



**Features**

      Create and manage daily tasks
      
      Organize tasks by priority or deadline
      
      Mark tasks as completed
      
      View upcoming tasks in a clear layout
      
      Receive reminders for important deadlines



**Benefits**

      Helps users stay organized and on track
      
      Reduces stress by keeping all tasks in one place
      
      Improves time management and productivity
      
      Makes it easier to balance school and personal life
      
      Provides a simple system that’s easy to learn and use
